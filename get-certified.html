Choose your pathway

Certification built for forests and businesses.

PEFC certification is assessed by independent accredited bodies. It supports sustainable forest management and traceable sourcing of eligible forest-based materials.

Start with the activity you want to certify: managing a forest, handling forest-based products or sourcing materials for a defined project.

  • Forest ManagementFor forest owners and managers.
  • Chain of CustodyFor businesses handling forest-based products.
  • Project SourcingFor defined projects tracking eligible forest-based materials under specialized requirements.
Certification options

Choose the certification that fits.

Forest Management and Chain of Custody are the two main pathways. Project Sourcing applies specialized chain of custody requirements to defined projects.

01

Forest Management

Independent assessment of forest management against a PEFC-endorsed national standard.

02

Chain of Custody

Track eligible forest-based material and support accurate PEFC claims throughout the supply chain.

03 · Specialized

Project Sourcing

Apply PEFC project sourcing requirements to specified projects and their forest-based material flows.
