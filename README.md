# PEFC International — Two-Page Demonstration Website

## What is included
- `index.html` — PEFC International homepage
- `get-certified.html` — certification-focused demonstration page
- `assets/img/pefc-logo.svg` — original user-supplied PEFC brand SVG
- `assets/img/compunnel-digital.svg` — designer credit supplied within the source template
- `assets/img/favicon-32.png` and `favicon-180.png` — favicon based on the supplied PEFC logo

## Open the website
Unzip the package and open `PEFC_International_Website_Rev2/index.html`. Navigation links connect the two pages. No installation is needed.

## Design decisions
- The original Port of Centralia template's section layout, grids, card arrangements, header, footer, and animations are retained.
- PEFC greens `#7BC11B` and `#97E245` remain the primary brand colors, with a restrained orange `#FF5D00` accent.
- Header and footer backgrounds are black, with a more visible rounded header outline. The large footer wordmark reads FOREST CERTIFICATION in bold uppercase lettering, and the Resources footer column has a one-word title.
- The Certification Pathways chips use one consistent green style; the featured Get Certified image shows a forest fieldwork scene.
- The PEFC Approach interactive topics use meaningful subject labels rather than inherited itinerary day labels, with an improved top-to-bottom text-protection gradient and a stronger Explore related topics heading.
- The Global Commitment horizontal story-card headings are white and higher contrast; hero number badges remain readable.
- Original PEFC SVG logo and designer-credit SVG are now included locally.
- Stock visuals use Pexels CDN links. **An internet connection is required to display photos and the hero video.**
- Demo content and enquiry forms are not connected to a production CMS or mailing service.
